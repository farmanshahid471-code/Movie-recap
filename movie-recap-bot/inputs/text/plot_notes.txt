PLOT NOTES — dark Pinocchio-style film (for a recap channel)

A 14-year-old boy named James loses both his parents in a car accident and goes to live
with his grandfather, Japetto. James makes two friends in the neighborhood, but one dies
of cancer and the other moves away. That day, Japetto decides James should never lose
anyone again, and begins spending long weeks hidden in the basement, which James isn't
allowed to enter.

Japetto finishes a creation that will never abandon James. One evening he lures James out
for fresh air, then promises to show him something "very special" tomorrow. That night
James wakes to a noise, chases a figure, and watches in shock as a living puppet named
Pinocchio emerges from a vine-covered hole in the wall. Pinocchio wants to be friends;
James is terrified, calling him a creepy thing. Japetto asks James to keep Pinocchio a
secret.

Over the weekend James and Pinocchio play and become best friends. Pinocchio watches a
documentary about animals protecting their herds and decides James is his herd. He asks
why he has no "member"; Japetto refuses to answer. One day Pinocchio discovers another
puppet in the basement, the Wooden Mother, who claims Japetto is hiding something. She
tells Pinocchio that to become a real boy he needs skin, blood, teeth, and hair — he must
take them from others and wear them. A cricket in the shadows overhears everything.

Pinocchio breaks out and asks to go to school with James. James sneaks him into school in
a bag, replacing him with the extra puppet. At school Pinocchio gets loose, and a woman
on a treadmill falls and is killed by falling weights. Later, bullies Trey, Logan, and
their girlfriends mock James and destroy his toys. James says he wishes the teens were
dead, and Pinocchio hears him.

At the dentist, Pinocchio watches James in pain. On the ride home a distracted driver
crashes the car and James is hospitalized. Pinocchio learns the crash was caused by a
drunk driver who has hurt many people. To protect James, Pinocchio kills the drunk driver,
ripping out his organs to make himself real. Then he kills the dentist, taking her teeth.

Meanwhile the kids watch old tapes and learn Japetto's secret: he was trying to cheat
death by trapping souls in puppets. James realizes Pinocchio is now going after everyone
who hurt him. Pinocchio attacks Logan's house: he kills Georgia and Logan at the pool,
then hunts Amanda and Trey. He kills them horribly, believing he's protecting James.

James and his friends try to stop Pinocchio, but Pinocchio thinks they are telling him to
kill more. Japetto finally sees that Pinocchio has escaped. The story ends on a dark,
unresolved note — Pinocchio is obsessed with becoming a real boy and "protecting" James,
no matter how many people die.

END OF PLOT NOTES
