A fourteen-year-old boy named James loses both of his parents in a terrible car accident.
He moves in with his grandfather, Japetto.
James quickly makes two friends in the neighborhood, but one of them dies of cancer.
The other friend moves away to a different town.
On that very day, Japetto decides that James should never lose anyone again.
He spends weeks locked away in the basement, where James is never allowed to go.
After countless hours down there, Japetto finishes a creation that will never abandon his grandson.
One evening, Japetto takes James out for some fresh air.
He promises to show him something very special the next day.
That night, James wakes up to a strange noise in the house.
He sees a figure in the shadows and chases it down to the basement.
To his shock, a living puppet named Pinocchio emerges to greet him.
James is terrified and calls him a creepy thing, but Japetto insists he is a real living being.
Japetto begs James to keep Pinocchio a secret.
Over the weekend, the two boys play together and become the best of friends.
Pinocchio watches a documentary about animals protecting their herds.
He decides that James is his herd, and that he must protect him at all costs.
Later, Pinocchio discovers another puppet in the basement, the Wooden Mother.
She tells him that to become a real boy, he needs skin, blood, teeth, and hair.
He must take them from others and wear them every day.
A cricket hidden in the shadows hears every word.
Pinocchio asks James to take him to school.
James hides him in his bag and sneaks him past his grandfather.
At the gym, Pinocchio slips out and watches the adults exercise.
A woman notices him, falls from her treadmill, and is crushed by the falling weights.
Then the bullies, Trey and Logan, find James and destroy his toys.
They mock him and threaten to kill him if he ever tells anyone.
James whispers that he wishes all those teens were dead, and Pinocchio hears him.
That night, Pinocchio decides to make every one of James' wishes come true.
At the dentist, Pinocchio watches James suffer in pain.
On the way home, a distracted driver crashes the car, and James ends up in the hospital.
Pinocchio learns that the crash was caused by a drunk driver who has hurt many people.
To protect his best friend, Pinocchio hunts the drunk driver down.
He rips the organs out of the man to make himself real.
Then he goes after the dentist and takes her teeth.
James and his friends watch old tapes and uncover the truth about Japetto.
Their grandfather was trying to cheat death by trapping souls in puppets.
James realizes that Pinocchio is now hunting everyone who ever hurt him.
Pinocchio attacks Logan's house and kills his friends at the swimming pool.
He hunts down Amanda, and then Trey, ending them in horrifying ways.
James begs Pinocchio to stop, but Pinocchio thinks he wants him to kill more.
By the time Japetto returns home, Pinocchio is gone.
James realizes the puppet will never stop trying to protect him.
And that is the story of a boy who brought a monster to life.
